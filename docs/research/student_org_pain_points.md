# USC Student Organization and Campus Community/Services Pain Points (2024-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-13. USC = University of Southern California. Method note: pages were read through a fetch tool that summarizes page text, so figures below are as reported by that tool from the cited page; anything taken only from a search-result snippet is marked "(snippet only)". Reddit (r/USC) could not be accessed at all by the search/fetch tools, so there is no first-hand Reddit evidence in these notes.

## 1. How do USC student orgs operate administratively (recognition, EngageSC, events, rooms, fairs), and where does it hurt?

### Takeaway
Annual RSO re-recognition, run by Campus Activities through EngageSC, is the best-documented and most repeated pain point: it broke down in Fall 2024 (900+ orgs, missed deadlines, rejections over trivial paperwork) and still produced multi-month delays in Fall 2025. Recognition gates everything else (rooms, USG/GSG funding, credit union account, involvement fair), so a paperwork failure stops a club from operating.

### Cited Findings
**Recognition / re-registration (owner: Campus Activities, Student Life; platform: EngageSC)**
- USC requires all RSOs to reapply for recognition every year; officers submit a member list, constitution and bylaws, and officers/advisors communicate with Campus Activities through EngageSC messaging. — [Daily Trojan, Nov 25 2025](https://dailytrojan.com/2025/11/25/student-organization-applications-still-being-processed-university-says/)
- Without recognition a group cannot access campus facilities such as classrooms, receive student government funding, or table at the involvement fair. — [Daily Trojan, Nov 25 2025](https://dailytrojan.com/2025/11/25/student-organization-applications-still-being-processed-university-says/)
- Fall 2025: Chavurah registered Aug 21 and got its recognition email Nov 12 (almost three months); its president said "Something with EngageSC glitched on [the advisor's] end" so messages never reached the advisor. CSSA was held up over a prior violation and slow advisor communication ("We were trying to reach out to them every week or even daily"). USC said only that "the majority of applications have been approved." — [Daily Trojan, Nov 25 2025](https://dailytrojan.com/2025/11/25/student-organization-applications-still-being-processed-university-says/)
- Fall 2024: 911 RSOs had to re-apply; forms advertised for July 1 were not sent until Aug 8; the constitution template grew from 9 to 14 pages; a login problem blocked access to mandatory training modules; missing signatures or initials caused rejection. One president called it "bureaucratically insane." — [Annenberg Media, Sep 12 2024](https://www.uscannenbergmedia.com/2024/09/12/confusion-reigns-among-usc-student-organizations-with-a-changing-recognition-process/)
- Fall 2024: clubs had about four days between training modules being posted and the first involvement-fair deadline; Garden Club could not find the training modules via Campus Activities and found them through Reddit; help requests took multiple days. Campus Activities replaced the two involvement fairs with small weekly "RSO Spotlight" tabling (100-150 groups vs 1,000+ expected, per a club leader). — [Daily Trojan, Sep 16 2024](https://dailytrojan.com/2024/09/16/rso-application-process-racked-with-delays/)
- Spoiler Alert Improv was denied for three missing words in its constitution, then rejected again based on outdated information; its adviser was disqualified because the application would not accept part-time employees. As of Sep 14 2024 the office had processed applications only through Aug 23. — [Daily Trojan Magazine, Oct 1 2024](https://dailytrojan.com/2024/10/01/clubs-persist-despite-setbacks/)
- At a two-hour USG open forum, 10+ RSOs complained; USC Political Union's president could not get a room or access the club's USC Credit Union account and was paying for events out of pocket; one speaker cited "a complete lack of communication and transparency." The Campus Activities staff responsible for recognition did not attend. — [Daily Trojan, Sep 18 2024](https://dailytrojan.com/2024/09/18/rso-representatives-speak-at-usg-senate-air-complaints/)
- 2026 presidential ticket Huang/Hammond campaigned on overhauling the RSO recognition process, which they called complicated and slow (they did not win). — [Daily Trojan candidate profiles, Feb 13 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/02/13/2026-usg-presidential-candidate-profiles/)
- For 2026-27, returning groups had to submit recognition by a priority deadline of Aug 8 2026 to be considered for the Aug 25-27 Fall Involvement Fair. — [Campus Activities, Involvement Fair & Tabling](https://campusactivities.usc.edu/?p=11343)

**Events, rooms, permits, tabling (owners: Trojan Event Services (TES), Campus Activities, DPS, FMS, USC Hospitality)**
- Room requests go through the Trojan Event Services website; classroom requests use a separate Classroom Scheduling form and are only accepted starting Week 4. — [Campus Activities RSO Resources](https://campusactivities.usc.edu/programs/recognized-student-organizations/rso-resources/)
- Fundraising events need a USC Events Permit Application (UEPA) plus a meeting with Campus Activities at least two weeks ahead; merchandise must come from licensed vendors. — [Campus Activities RSO Resources](https://campusactivities.usc.edu/programs/recognized-student-organizations/rso-resources/)
- TES terms require contacting DPS at least two weeks before an event; DPS decides on fees and special security (snippet only). — [TES Terms and Conditions 2026](https://trojanevents.usc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/TES-Terms-and-Conditions-2026.pdf)
- Permit approval requires a diagram and event timeline four weeks before the event date (snippet only). — [USC Event Calendar, Reserving Space](https://calendar.usc.edu/event-planning)
- TES maintains an RSO violation intake form that includes "events not registered/reserved properly" (snippet only). — [TES RSO Violation Report form](https://trojanevents.usc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/RSO-Violation-Report.pdf)
- Trousdale information tables are reserved through TES on weekdays; tabling for fundraising or other purposes needs a space reservation plus a UEPA. — [Campus Activities, Involvement Fair & Tabling](https://campusactivities.usc.edu/?p=11343)
- Club money sits in USC Credit Union accounts; a rec club said withdrawals need paperwork through the Recreational Club Council, making it hesitant to collect dues. — [Annenberg Media, Sep 12 2024](https://www.uscannenbergmedia.com/2024/09/12/confusion-reigns-among-usc-student-organizations-with-a-changing-recognition-process/)
- USG and Campus Activities run an "FMS RSO Cost Waiver Fund" to waive Facilities Management Services event costs. — [USG RSO resources](https://usg.usc.edu/resources/rsos/)

**RSO counts conflict:** 911 (Sep 2024) — [Annenberg Media](https://www.uscannenbergmedia.com/2024/09/12/confusion-reigns-among-usc-student-organizations-with-a-changing-recognition-process/); "over a thousand" (2023 USG quote) — [Annenberg Media](https://www.uscannenbergmedia.com/2023/04/29/usg-rejected-funding-for-established-student-organizations/); "more than 800" (Campus Activities site, snippet only) — [Campus Activities](https://campusactivities.usc.edu/); "over 600" (Oct 2026) — [Annenberg Media](https://www.uscannenbergmedia.com/2026/10/07/usc-zixun-shetuan-jingzheng-bairehua-shenqing/).

### Inferences
- An officer planning one event touches at least five separately owned systems (EngageSC, TES reservation, UEPA via DPS, Hospitality food form, USG/GSG funding) with different lead times (2, 4, 6, 9 weeks). No source shows a single consolidated timeline or checklist; a "what do I need and by when" planner is an evident gap.
- Recognition failures are largely clerical (missing words, signatures, wrong advisor type, unread messages). A pre-submission checker against the constitution template and checklist is technically simple and addresses the documented failure mode, but the process owner is Campus Activities, not USG.
- The falling RSO count (911 to ~600-800) is consistent with attrition from the recognition process, but no source states the cause.

### Gaps
- No first-hand officer complaints about room reservation, catering or DPS fees specifically (2024-2026); Reddit was inaccessible. The process rules are documented, the pain is not.
- No data on Fall 2026 recognition performance (how many pending, how long).
- Campus Activities staffing levels and whether layoffs hit that office: not found.

## 2. USG and GSG funding for student orgs: process and documented complaints

### Takeaway
USG funding (about $350-390K per year for RSOs, applied for in EngageSC) is rule-dense, first-come-first-served, pays vendors directly with no reimbursements, auto-denies on missed deadlines or unfixed errors within 48 hours, and has no written appeal path. USG amends the guidelines several times a year, itself citing "confusion with the wording". This is the strongest USG-owned problem area found.

### Cited Findings
**How USG funding works (owner: USG Funding Department under the CFO; processing by Campus Activities professional staff)**
- Four boards: Professional & Academic, Philanthropy, Social & Recreation/Startup, Performing & Visual Arts. Caps: 4 funded events per RSO per semester, $8,000 per RSO per year. Applications close Wednesdays 11:59 PM, are filed in EngageSC, and "funding is not guaranteed." — [USG Funding page](https://usg.usc.edu/branches/funding/)
- 2026-27 guidelines (current as of Oct 21 2026): vendor purchases due six academic weeks, travel nine academic weeks, before the initial payment due date; missed deadline = automatic denial; incomplete applications must be fixed within 2 business days or are denied; each expense needs a quote, cart screenshots with all fees, Amazon wish-list links; post-event evaluation with photos and final invoices due within 3 business days; food capped at $35 per person, hotels $400 per night. — [USG 26-27 Funding Department Guidelines](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1noJFJ4SxrFGD1Zm-6EqUHpXSIOUuFIL-gIobWXcXCt0/edit?usp=sharing)
- Ineligible: reimbursements, gift cards, durable goods, restaurant dining, rideshare/buses/vans/car rentals, peer-to-peer payments (Airbnb, Zelle), talent/performer payments, ticketed events, international travel, balloons. Vendors not on the list are not accepted, though the list is described as non-exhaustive. No appeal process is described. — [USG 26-27 Funding Department Guidelines](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1noJFJ4SxrFGD1Zm-6EqUHpXSIOUuFIL-gIobWXcXCt0/edit?usp=sharing)
- Review chain: board Director and Associate Director, then Graduate Advisors and Campus Activities professional staff, then the Funding Director updates status. The CFO can close a board that hits 50% utilization before the end of fall. — [USG 26-27 Funding Department Guidelines](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1noJFJ4SxrFGD1Zm-6EqUHpXSIOUuFIL-gIobWXcXCt0/edit?usp=sharing)
- FY26 results: $350,000 initial, $362,000 after reallocation, 106 events funded across four boards (30/34/10/32), boards 93-105% utilized. The report gives no application, denial or processing-time counts. — [USG FY26 Financial Report](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1NMhd0zbUC6XG-XZvpWI82reC8f_JR6Miw5krWBPDsfQ/edit?usp=sharing)
- FY27: total USG budget $2.6M; funding department $388,000 (+6.7%). — [Daily Trojan, Apr 29 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/?p=255781). The USG funding page separately states "up to $356,100" for the school year — [USG Funding page](https://usg.usc.edu/branches/funding/) (figures conflict; the page may be stale).

**Rule churn and transparency**
- Until July 2025 denials came without reasons and events over $4,000 were automatically rejected; CFO Avery Horton added mandatory denial reasons and case-by-case review. — [Daily Trojan, Aug 20 2025](https://dailytrojan.com/2025/08/20/usg-makes-funding-decisions-more-transparent/)
- Guideline amendments in 14 months: SB 145-05 (Sep 2025, travel deadline 8 to 9 weeks) — [Daily Trojan, Oct 1 2025](https://dailytrojan.com/2025/10/01/usg-hears-presentations-on-funding-student-support/); SB 146-02 (Aug 2026, traveler name/DOB/USC ID required; a senator asked whether the form itself would reflect it and the answer suggested not) — [Daily Trojan, Aug 26 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/08/26/usg-08-26/); SB 146-09 (Oct 2026, nine-week rule reworded to "clear up some confusion with the wording") — [Daily Trojan, Oct 28 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/10/28/usg-approves-resolution-supporting-international-students-curricular-practical-training/)
- A single funding workshop drew 50+ RSO leaders with questions about the process. — [Daily Trojan, Oct 1 2025](https://dailytrojan.com/2025/10/01/usg-hears-presentations-on-funding-student-support/)
- From FY27, "membership organizations" can no longer get money through programming assemblies and must apply through the general funding boards; officials attributed it to federal DEI policy but named no specific rule. Academic Culture Assembly was cut 38.1%. — [Daily Trojan, Apr 29 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/?p=255781)
- FY26 budget cut programming assemblies about 12% (snippet only) — [Daily Trojan](https://dailytrojan.com/?p=233553); $155,400 was restored at an emergency meeting in Nov 2025 — [Daily Trojan, Nov 13 2025](https://dailytrojan.com/2025/11/13/usg-senate-allocates-more-than-155000-in-new-funds-to-committees-assemblies/)
- Older but structurally identical (2023): USG funded 166 of 378 applicants; applications closed two weeks early without notice; a dance group learned of denial three weeks before a $4,500 Bovard deposit; applicants had to submit a PowerPoint, spreadsheet, vendor quotes and a Google form. — [Annenberg Media, Apr 29 2023](https://www.uscannenbergmedia.com/2023/04/29/usg-rejected-funding-for-established-student-organizations/)
- Fall 2024: the USG funding window opened late and closed under eight hours later. — [Daily Trojan, Sep 16 2024](https://dailytrojan.com/2024/09/16/rso-application-process-racked-with-delays/)
- President Rahman reported expanding the USG RSO liaison role to four positions (Oct 2026). — [Daily Trojan, Oct 28 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/10/28/usg-approves-resolution-supporting-international-students-curricular-practical-training/)

**GSG funding (owner: GSG Finance Committee; five finance directors on the 2026-27 board)**
- Funded by the graduate Student Programming Fee ($40 per semester; online students $20 and online RSOs are ineligible) (snippet only). — [GSG Funding for RSOs](https://gsg.usc.edu/?p=21392)
- Process: mandatory finance orientation video, pass a Finance Assessment to unlock the application, then apply in EngageSC 6-8 weeks before the event depending on type; events must be open to all graduate students; extra forms (vendor list, flyer template, constituency approval memo, transportation waiver, prize recipient form). — [GSG Funding for RSOs](https://gsg.usc.edu/?p=21392)
- GSG represents 27,500+ graduate and professional students; 2026-27 president is Janielle Cuala. — [GSG site](https://gsg.usc.edu/)

### Inferences
- The guidelines contain traps a tool could catch before submission: ineligible item types, unlisted vendors, per-person food cap, wrong lead time, missing quotes. Because incomplete or late applications are denied outright and funds are first-come-first-served, a pre-check has direct dollar value to clubs and reduces rework for the volunteer boards and Campus Activities staff.
- USG publishes outcomes (106 events) but not application volume, denial reasons or turnaround, so it cannot currently show where applicants fail. A tool that logs structured denial reasons would give USG data it lacks.
- The FY27 redirect of member organizations to the general boards likely raises application volume on those boards in 2026-27; no source quantifies it.
- Clubs that need money from both USG and GSG face two rule sets, two trainings and two calendars in the same platform.

### Gaps
- "Reimbursement takes months" could not be evidenced. USG forbids reimbursements and pays vendors directly; no 2024-2026 article documents payment delays. Reddit may hold such complaints but was inaccessible.
- No public count of USG applications received/denied for FY25-FY27, and no published turnaround times.
- GSG caps, eligible expenses, payment method and budget totals were not on the public page (they sit in a guidebook not retrieved). No 2025-2026 press coverage of GSG funding disputes was found.

## 3. Club recruitment and discovery; application management; officer transitions

### Takeaway
Selective-club culture is heavily covered and resented: consulting, finance and some service clubs still run essays, multiple interview rounds and coffee chats despite a 2024 open-membership rule, and rejected applicants get no feedback. Evidence on how clubs manage applications internally and on officer-transition knowledge loss is thin.

### Cited Findings
- Since Fall 2024 all RSOs must be open to interested students; Marshall clubs responded with "general membership" tiers while keeping selective programs. A freshman: "The general membership has not necessarily opened the doors it should." Value Investing Group said Marshall gave "not a strict rubric or guideline." — [Daily Trojan, Oct 18 2024](https://dailytrojan.com/2024/10/18/new-rules-force-marshall-clubs-to-open-up-membership/)
- 2025 rule update: RSOs must admit eligible students, staff, faculty and alumni as non-voting members; multi-round interviews continue at clubs such as Trojan Consulting Group. — [Annenberg Media, Sep 11 2025](https://www.uscannenbergmedia.com/2025/09/11/are-multi-round-interviews-for-business-school-clubs-a-thing-of-the-past-many-usc-students-think-not/)
- Oct 2026: 10+ consulting clubs use written tests and multiple interview rounds; applicants are informally expected to hold coffee chats with about three members; a rejected sophomore got no feedback and no path to stay involved; some general-member programs charge dues for little value; the open Consulting Consortium shut down Jan 6 2026. — [Annenberg Media (Chinese-language), Oct 7 2026](https://www.uscannenbergmedia.com/2026/10/07/usc-zixun-shetuan-jingzheng-bairehua-shenqing/)
- A social food club ran essays plus a group "vibe check" interview; service groups require essays, interviews and knowledge tests. USC senior: "There's no way I have to write an essay just to get into a club for socialization." — [EdSource via Piedmont Exedra, Jan 7 2025](https://piedmontexedra.com/2025/01/college-clubs-becoming-just-as-competitive-as-getting-into-college)
- Some clubs "can require over three rounds of written applications, socials, and interviews" (Innovative Design, LavaLab, Haute, Marshall consulting orgs) (snippet only). — [Trojans360](https://www.trojans360.com/trojans360posts/club-culture-and-recruiting-season)
- Alpha Phi Omega gets 50-75 applicants a semester and takes about 25; Helenes recruitment includes two weeks of events, application, group interview and an exam (snippet only). — [Daily Trojan Magazine](https://dailytrojan.com/?p=217063)
- Discovery depends on the involvement fair: when clubs missed it in 2024, "people just don't know they exist." — [Annenberg Media, Sep 12 2024](https://www.uscannenbergmedia.com/2024/09/12/confusion-reigns-among-usc-student-organizations-with-a-changing-recognition-process/)
- USG itself received 211 applications in Fall 2026 recruitment, credited to involvement-fair and tabling outreach. — [Daily Trojan, Sep 9 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/09/09/usg-amends-senate-meeting-rules-announces-summer-highlights/)
- USG's chief of staff is writing a how-to guidebook for USG officers (a transition-knowledge fix inside USG). — [Daily Trojan, Oct 28 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/10/28/usg-approves-resolution-supporting-international-students-curricular-practical-training/)

### Inferences
- Two tool-shaped gaps: (a) matching students to open clubs among hundreds of RSOs beyond a three-day fair; (b) structured, low-effort feedback to rejected applicants. Both are owned by clubs and Campus Activities rather than student government.
- Annual re-recognition plus yearly officer turnover means each new board relearns recognition, funding and event rules from scratch; the 2024 failures (cannot find trainings, wrong constitution wording) are what knowledge loss looks like, though no source frames it that way.

### Gaps
- No source documents the tools clubs use to run applications (Google Forms, spreadsheets) or their problems.
- No source documents officer-transition knowledge loss in RSOs directly.
- No acceptance-rate data for USC clubs; the Nov 6 2025 Daily Trojan Magazine piece on club recruitment returned a 404.

## 4. Event discovery

### Takeaway
Event information is split across EngageSC, calendar.usc.edu, school calendars, Instagram, newsletters and physical boards, and a student guide says Instagram is the reliable channel. Direct, recent evidence of student frustration with event discovery was not found, so this is a plausible but weakly evidenced pain point.

### Cited Findings
- EngageSC is the official hub for events and organizations; Campus Activities says student organizations "are responsible for the majority of programs and events held on campus." — [Daily Trojan Magazine, Oct 1 2024](https://dailytrojan.com/2024/10/01/clubs-persist-despite-setbacks/)
- A student-run guide says the best way to stay current is each group's Instagram, and that events have limited registration and fill up fast (snippet only). — [Trojans360](https://www.trojans360.com/trojans360posts/your-guide-to-attending-free-events-on-campus)
- Campus Activities guidance tells organizers to post on both EngageSC and calendar.usc.edu for visibility (snippet only). — [EngageSC Calendar How-to](https://campusactivities.usc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/11/EngageSC-Calendar-How-to.pdf)
- Older guide (circa 2018) lists seven separate channels including Facebook pages, the ResX app, calendar.usc.edu, Visions and Voices and a physical blackboard outside TCC. — [Trojans360](https://www.trojans360.com/trojans360posts/where-to-find-free-events-usc)
- Free food: "Tommy's Table" redistributes surplus food from private campus events to food-insecure students. — [Daily Trojan, Mar 27 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/03/27/food-affordability-on-campus-concerns/)
- USG senators use weekly Instagram takeovers to publicize their own work. — [Daily Trojan, Feb 13 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/02/13/usg-leaders-laud-progress-in-past-term/)

### Inferences
- Clubs double- or triple-post (EngageSC for compliance, Instagram for reach), so an aggregator that reads public Instagram/EngageSC listings would reduce work on both sides; nothing found shows USC or USG operating one.

### Gaps
- No 2024-2026 article, survey or statistic on students missing events or disliking EngageSC for discovery. No Sidechat/Fizz evidence. No EngageSC usage numbers.

## 5. Campus services and information friction

### Takeaway
The clearest 2025-2026 service failures are academic advising after mass advisor layoffs (students unable to book before registration), opaque financial aid information (USG is creating a liaison because FAQs are confusing), low awareness of basic-needs resources, and a portal landscape in mid-migration. USC has a campus-wide ChatGPT Edu license and a mental-health chatbot but no general "which office handles this" assistant was found.

### Cited Findings
- Advising: Dornsife laid off 162 staff including "nearly all" advisers and Viterbi all 57 advisers (Oct 2025). In Spring 2026 a sophomore found every adviser on Advise USC booked for the semester and got an appointment only after her registration date; a junior met an adviser two weeks after his date and could not use drop-ins held during work hours. Dornsife offered virtual drop-ins and an online course-review form. — [Daily Trojan, May 1 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/05/01/layoffs-delay-dornsife-viterbi-student-registration/)
- Financial aid: USG's Affordability and Basic Needs Committee is creating a financial aid liaison because existing FAQs are hard to follow ("the wording sometimes is a little confusing"). A senator also advocated for a financial aid student worker. — [Daily Trojan, Oct 21 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/10/21/usg-to-create-a-financial-aid-liaison-position/); [Daily Trojan, Sep 9 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/09/09/usg-amends-senate-meeting-rules-announces-summer-highlights/)
- Basic needs: "Even if there are resources, it's not widely known" (student who learned of the Trojan Food Pantry only via First Gen Plus staff). Pantry excludes students with a dining-hall plan; CalFresh offers up to $292 per month with complex eligibility; USG runs 15-minute EBT sign-up workshops. — [Daily Trojan, Mar 27 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/03/27/food-affordability-on-campus-concerns/); [Daily Trojan, Oct 21 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/10/21/usg-to-create-a-financial-aid-liaison-position/)
- International students: USG Resolution 146-10 asks the Office of International Services and departments for clear CPT timelines after an Aug 24 2026 federal change. — [Daily Trojan, Oct 28 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/10/28/usg-approves-resolution-supporting-international-students-curricular-practical-training/)
- Portals: OASIS retired June 3 2026 and myUSC is set to retire in 2027, both replaced by "Experience USC"; the interim CIO said there was "no immediate transition planned for myUSC's services." myUSC currently fronts Brightspace, WebReg and USCe.pay. — [Daily Trojan, May 4 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/05/04/myusc-for-students-set-to-retire-in-2027/)
- Existing AI: ChatGPT Edu opened to all students, faculty and staff in January 2026. — [USC announcement](https://we-are.usc.edu/?p=15721); cost reported at $3.1M per year (headline, snippet only) — [Daily Trojan, Jan 22 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/01/22/__trashed-3/). USC Student Health has an AI mental-health chatbot, "Ask Ari" (vendor case study, snippet only) — [Toptal case study](https://www.casestudies.com/company/toptal/case-study/usc-launches-ai-mental-health-chatbot-for-40000-students-with-toptal)
- Existing apps: USC Gateway for Mobile is a quick-links page (maps, directory, myUSC, tram schedule) (snippet only) — [mobile.usc.edu](https://mobile.usc.edu/); the USC Campus Dining app gained estimated wait times for TCC restaurants after USG advocacy — [Daily Trojan fact check](https://dailytrojan.com/?p=249660)
- Transportation: free shared Lyft ("Fryft") start moved from 6 to 7 p.m. in Fall 2025 while the transportation fee rose from $117 to $146 per semester; a student now waits an extra hour to get groceries. — [Daily Trojan, Aug 29 2025](https://dailytrojan.com/2025/08/29/fryft-hours-reduced-starting-at-7-p-m/). A petition by Lorenzo residents called the free Lyft service unreliable and unsafe (86 signatures). — [Daily Trojan, Sep 9 2025](https://dailytrojan.com/?p=238065). USC Transportation told a senator there was no money for a Waymo program. — [Daily Trojan, Feb 13 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/02/13/on-paper-usg-senators-had-a-slow-year-we-asked-them-why/)
- Campus access: daytime ID scanning ended in 2025 (headline, snippet only) — [Daily Trojan](https://dailytrojan.com/?p=236334); the Watt Way gate stopped being 24/7 on Aug 1 2025, drawing safety and commute complaints (snippet only) — [Daily Trojan, Sep 29 2025](https://dailytrojan.com/2025/09/29/watt-way-entrance-no-longer-24-7/); Secret Service "stop and search" zones are planned for LA28 (headline, snippet only) — [Daily Trojan](https://dailytrojan.com/?p=252706)
- Dining: unused dining dollars are forfeited each semester; USG's chief diversity officer is seeking rollover and use at USC Village. Flex120 cost $2,074 per semester in 2025-26. — [Daily Trojan, Oct 21 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/10/21/usg-to-create-a-financial-aid-liaison-position/); [Daily Trojan, Mar 27 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/03/27/food-affordability-on-campus-concerns/)
- Safety: dozens of scooter/personal-mobility crashes logged in 2026 (teaser only); a senator is collecting data on how safe students feel. — [Daily Trojan USG index](https://dailytrojan.com/news/usg/); [Daily Trojan, Oct 28 2026](https://dailytrojan.com/2026/10/28/usg-approves-resolution-supporting-international-students-curricular-practical-training/)

### Inferences
- Advising, financial aid and basic needs share one pattern: the information exists but is hard to parse or find, and the humans who used to explain it were cut or are overbooked. USG is answering with people (liaisons, workshops); a question-answering layer over official pages is the automation analogue, and USC already licenses ChatGPT Edu.
- The 2026-27 portal migration (OASIS gone, myUSC going) is a live source of "where is X now" confusion, though no article quotes students on it.

### Gaps

### Takeaway
