    Trousdale Parkway and Gavin Herbert Plaza Information Tables

    Definition of Tabling: A table located in an approved campus outdoor area to distribute information, promote events, recruit, and/or fundraise.

    • Only recognized student organizations can table on USC Campuses.

    USC Recognized Student Organization (RSO) Tabling Guidelines

    • RSO Tabling may only happen in specific outdoor on campus locations between the hours of 10:00 a.m. – 2:00 p.m. Monday through Friday.
      • At UPC, RSO Tabling is only allowed on Trousdale Parkway along the western edge of Alumni Park and Gavin Herbert Plaza. Tables cannot be set up on the grassy median or Bovard (west) side of Trousdale. Tabling is NOT allowed at the USC Village.
    • A valid permit is required for RSO Tabling on Trousdale Parkway or Gavin Herbert Plaza.
    • RSO Tabling is not permitted during official university functions or holidays including but not limited to Welcome Week, Orientation, and home football game days, or when there is a reservation that is using all of Trousdale. For example, a Career Fair.
    • Only RSOs are permitted to table on campus for the purpose of promoting their RSO or hosting an event.
    • The RSO table must be used for display purposes only. If it’s a student organization sponsored fundraiser, the organization must submit a university event permit application for fundraising approval. Please note that a reservation number (provided by Trojan Event Services) is needed to get the event permit.
    • RSO Tabling set up may only include a table and 2 chairs only. No canopy or tent.
    • There is a limit of 5 tables and 10 chairs to be borrowed from SKS per day.
    • No activations.
    • No amplification.
    • Permit is non-transferable.

    Tabling FAQ

    Where do I get the RSO Tabling Permit? Go to the Concierge Desk on the 1st floor of the Steven and Kathryn Sample (SKS) building. Provide your student ID and name of the student organization that you are tabling for. The student ID must be left at the front desk.

    Where do I pick up my table? All you need to do is check in with the Campus Center Consultants located at the front desk of the SKS building. You will need to leave a USC Student ID with the staff to check out your table and take it to Trousdale Parkway or Gavin Herbert Plaza.

    If I have my own table and chairs, do I still need a permit? Yes, RSO Tabling permits are required even if you have your own table/chairs. The permit must be displayed on your table at all times.

    Do I have to return my permit, table and/or chairs? Yes. Please return the permit, table and/or chair(s) to the front desk by 2:15 p.m. Failing to return the permit, tables and/or chairs could result in a suspension of tabling privileges or a fine equal to the cost of replacement. Upon return, your student ID will be given back to you.

    How many days can my student organization table?  You can table for two (2) consecutive days per week. You can not exceed six (6) days per month.
